name: Atomic Powers
level: 2
klass: adept
subclass: nuclear
mechanics:
  - type: power-augment
    damageTypes:
      - radiant
    damageBonus:
      type: proficiency
---
At 2nd level, when you learn a power, you can choose to learn an Atomic version of that power. Instead of dealing it's
normal damage, it now deals Radiant damage. You also add your proficiency modifier to powers that deal Radiant Damage.
