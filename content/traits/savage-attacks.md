---
name: Savage Attacks
species:
  - behemoth
mechanics:
  - type: attack-augment
    attackLimit:
      model: weapon
      type: melee
    augmentTypes:
      - damage
    addCritDie: 1
---
When you score a critical hit with a melee weapon attack, you can roll one of
the weapon’s damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage
of the critical hit.
