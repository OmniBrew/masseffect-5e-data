name: Mind-Addling Toxin
mechanics:
  - type: other
    dc:
      base: 8
      proficient: true
      mod: int
    shortDesc: "Forgo any number of Sneak Attack dice: on a failed save, the target has disadvantage on concentration checks for that many rounds."
type: sneak-toxin
---
You can choose to forego any number of sneak attack dice. If the target fails the saving throw, they have
disadvantage on any concentration check for the next number of rounds equal to the amount of dice you sacrificed.
