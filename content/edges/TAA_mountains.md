---
name: Mountains
mechanics:
  - type: fall-damage
    reduce:
      bonus:
        type: level
        value: tracker
        multiplier: 5
    note: take less falling damage equal to 5x your Tracker level
type: tracker-affinity-advancement
---
Your climbing speed now equals your movement speed (or is increased by :distance{length="10"} instead of :distance{length="5"} if you already had one.), and when falling, you take less damage equal to 5x your Tracker level.

While climbing a vertical surface, your hands are considered to be kept free, allowing you to attack with two-handed weapons and other such things that require a free hand.
